1. Some pros are that agile development gives you the ability to frequently revisit the planning stages
   to add new features as the customer is using the product and to address bugs as they come up rather
   than needing to go back to the early stages and start over. Some potential cons are since the software
   is in continuous development, the end user could end up using a broken version while waiting for a bug
   to be fixed. Another potential con is that it is a more complicated process than a simple plan-develop-test-release
   approach.

2. The product owner is the person that decides what to build and assembles the initial team, including the scrum master.
   The scrum master's role is to manage the development of the product by organizing meetings and sprints.
